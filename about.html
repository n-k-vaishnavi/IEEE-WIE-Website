ABOUT IEEE

Advancing Technology for Humanity

IEEE is the world's largest technical professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity.

IEEE brings together engineers, scientists, researchers, students and technology professionals through technical publications, conferences, standards, educational activities and professional communities.

Through its global network, IEEE provides opportunities for learning, collaboration, innovation and professional development.

IEEE WOMEN IN ENGINEERING

Connecting. Supporting. Inspiring.

IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) is a global network of IEEE members and volunteers dedicated to promoting women engineers and scientists and inspiring girls to pursue careers in engineering, science and technology.

IEEE WIE supports networking, mentoring, leadership development, professional growth, outreach and activities that encourage participation and retention in STEM fields.

At the student level, WIE provides opportunities for students to learn, connect with professionals, develop leadership skills and participate in technical and community-oriented activities.

IEEE BANGALORE SECTION

Our Regional IEEE Community

The IEEE Bangalore Section is part of the IEEE community in India and provides a platform for members, students, researchers and professionals to participate in technical, educational and professional activities.

The section was established in 1977 and has supported activities involving students, academia, industry and research communities.

Activities associated with the section include technical talks, student activities, leadership initiatives and programs that encourage innovation and engineering for societal benefit.

OUR CHAPTER

IEEE WIE JSSATEB

The IEEE WIE JSSATEB Chapter provides a student-focused platform for learning, collaboration, leadership and professional development in engineering and technology.

The chapter aims to create opportunities for students to develop technical knowledge, communication skills, leadership abilities and connections with peers and professionals.

Through workshops, technical activities, outreach, mentoring and community initiatives, the chapter works to encourage students to explore engineering and technology.

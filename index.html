WHO WE ARE

IEEE Women in Engineering

IEEE Women in Engineering is a global network dedicated to inspiring women and girls to pursue careers in engineering, science and technology.

Our student chapter at JSS Academy of Technical Education Bangalore aims to create opportunities for learning, leadership, collaboration and growth.

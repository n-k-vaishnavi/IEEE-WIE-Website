WHO WE ARE

IEEE Women in Engineering

IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) is a global network dedicated to inspiring women and girls to pursue and thrive in careers in engineering, science, and technology.

Our student chapter at JSS Academy of Technical Education, Bengaluru fosters an inclusive environment that provides hands-on technical workshops, professional mentorship, leadership development, and collaboration.
